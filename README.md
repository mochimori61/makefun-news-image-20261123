THE WORLD IN FULL — a one-image broadsheet of Monday 23 November 2026, drawn in
ivory, bottle-green ink and old gold. 38 stories from 7 regions in a single
1024x1024 page: 13 numbered front-page cards led by the Lisbon cost-of-living
march (with COP31 ending without a deal and the Kyiv boy pulled alive from the
rubble completing the lead trio), 25 also-on briefs, a THE DAY IN NUMBERS strip
(258-173 · 720/9d · 14 · 6 · $3,538 · 65.9%), a dotted world map with numbered
pins, and a red DATE CHECK box naming seven stories that were NOT 23 November
news. Every card carries a certainty chip (REPORTED / CLAIM / OFFICIAL /
ON RECORD / ONGOING / CONFIRMED) and a source line; contested claims are
attributed in the headlines themselves.
